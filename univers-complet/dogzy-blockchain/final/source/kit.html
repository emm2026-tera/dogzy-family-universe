One-page pitch

Play it. Earn it. Own it.

Dogzy Family Universe is an original illustrated world of dog characters, each with a profession, created by the Québec artist Valkyrie (Sania Labbé). It comes to life in Dogzie Town, a PlayStation VR game in development, and connects to a real digital token, DOGZY, on Avalanche.

The universe

25+ dog characters with professions: Chef (Golden Retriever), Barmaid (Cavalier), Police Officer (German Shepherd), Firefighter (Saint Bernard), Veterinarian (Labrador), Pilot (Weimaraner) and many more.

The progression

Players level up in each job. Level 5 of every job is a business: restaurant chain, bank, clinic, gallery, studio, airport. The first player to reach it owns it; later players buy a franchise. Characters wear equippable accessories.

The game

In Dogzie Town (PlayStation VR, in development), players earn in-game Dogzy tokens by working, building businesses and taking part in events.

The token

DOGZY is a standard ERC-20 token on Avalanche C-Chain: EVM-compatible, fast and low-fee. The DogzyBridge contract lets players move earned tokens out of the game into a wallet they control.

Two smart contracts

DogzyToken

The DOGZY token, built on the ERC-20 standard, the most widely used standard for digital tokens. Total supply and exchange rate are set at deployment; final tokenomics are [TBD].

DogzyBridge

Checks every voucher and pays the player from a DOGZY reserve funded by the project. Every exchange leaves a public, verifiable record on the blockchain.

Why Avalanche C-Chain

EVM-compatibleWorks with standard Ethereum tools and wallets (Core, MetaMask).
Fast, low feesSuited to frequent, small in-game exports.
Free testnetFuji allows full validation before any real launch.

Owner safeguards

Pause claims
Set the exchange rate
Replace the signing key
Cap each claim
Fund or withdraw the reserve

These settings can be changed only by the owner. The bridge can never pay out more DOGZY than the reserve holds.

Built-in protections

Single-use vouchers bound to one player, expiry dates, verified signatures, per-claim cap, reserve limit, emergency pause, owner-only settings, re-entrancy protection. Built on the widely used, audited OpenZeppelin libraries.

Main risk: the server signing key

Whoever holds it could issue vouchers. It must stay on the server only, never shared. If it ever leaked, the cap, the reserve limit and the pause limit the damage, and the owner can replace the key.

Questions and answers

Investor Q&A

01Why a token?

So players can take value they earned in the game out to a wallet they control, as real DOGZY tokens.

02Why Avalanche?

It works with Ethereum tools (EVM), transactions are fast, fees are low, and the free Fuji testnet allows full validation first.

03Can someone cheat or steal a voucher?

The 12 automated tests confirm that reused, stolen, forged, altered and expired vouchers are rejected, along with claims above the cap or the reserve and admin actions by anyone but the owner (page 7).

04What is the biggest risk?

The server signing key. It must stay on the server only. Caps, the reserve limit and the emergency pause limit the damage if it ever leaks.

05Is it live? Has it been audited?

Not yet. It is a working prototype: it compiles and all tests pass. Next is the Fuji testnet, then an independent audit before mainnet. Auditor and budget: [TBD].

06What are the total supply and exchange rate?

Both are configurable at deployment. Final tokenomics: [TBD].

07Is the token for sale?

No. No token sale is open. Any future distribution would follow legal review: [TBD].

08What is the state of the game?

Dogzie Town is in development for PlayStation VR. Status, platform agreements and release date: [TBD].

09Is there a demo?

An interactive investor demo is in development and will be shared when ready.

10How do the businesses work?

Level 5 of each job is a business. The first player to reach it owns it; later players buy franchises.
