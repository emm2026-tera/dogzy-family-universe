Valkyrie Gallery: A New Collection Every Month

Each month, the Valkyrie Gallery unveils a brand-new collection of original paintings by the artist Valkyrie. Return every month to discover new works, new themes and new stories, many of which will become collectible NFTs in the Dogzy Family Universe.

Artistic Journey

Self-taught painter and multidisciplinary creator, Valkyrie has exhibited her work internationally in Paris, Milan, Berlin, Barcelona, Spain, India, Dubai (United Arab Emirates), São Paulo, Strasbourg, Zurich and Venice.

- 24 international exhibitions
- 14 curatorial selections
- 41,567 views on Singulart, among the top 10% most-viewed artists
- Selected by jury for the "Main Libre" exhibition, Centre des arts visuels
- Featured in Artist Closeup magazine
