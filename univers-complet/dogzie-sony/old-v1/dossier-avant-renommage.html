Executive summary

Dogzie Town in one page

01 · WHAT

A luxury open-world action game

GTA-style freedom, Burnout-style chases and Payday-style heists, in a town of dogs inspired by Dubai and Saint-Martin. It is the PlayStation game of the Dogzie Family Universe, an original IP that is 100 % owned by its creator.

02 · WHY NOW

The world is built. Production is next.

Characters, buildings, expansions, an original soundtrack and a concept trailer already exist, built for about $500 in cash plus the creator’s own time. The game is at the concept stage, so this is the moment when a partner’s input can still shape it.

03 · THE ASK

An exploratory conversation

About publishing, partnership or developer support for Phase 2, the PlayStation build. Funding target: $500K–$550K CAD. The structure is open: we want to understand Sony’s view first.

Core rule · one of each

Every mission includes one character from each faction. They confront each other, cooperate or cross paths in opposing or complementary roles.

Local or online, players are always in opposite camps. It is a built-in rivalry that works on the couch and online.

The Dogzy layer: optional, outside the console

On PlayStation, balances stay on the game server and no wallet is ever needed. In the wider universe, players could choose to withdraw tokens to a wallet with a one-time code and a bridge. We propose to build that layer on Soneium, Sony’s own blockchain, subject to PlayStation platform policy. On console it can be adapted, or removed entirely.

Contracts written, not deployed, not audited. No partnership, approval or integration with Soneium or Sony exists.

Dogzie Town en une page

Dogzie Town est le jeu console PlayStation du Dogzie Family Universe, une propriété intellectuelle originale créée par Valkyrie (Sania), au Québec. C’est un monde ouvert de luxe inspiré de Dubaï et de Saint-Martin, qui mélange l’esprit GTA, Burnout et Payday : voitures volées, tatouages, condo, poursuites avec des coups signature qui rapportent des points bonus, et braquages de banques et de bijouteries.

Neuf factions en deux camps se croisent dans chaque mission : le vol du collier de diamants au gala, le spectacle de la chanteuse… La progression va du personnage de base aux 10 accessoires, puis au statut de propriétaire ou de franchisé. Il n’y a pas de pay-to-win.

Couche numérique Dogzy : elle est optionnelle et hors de l’expérience console. Nous proposons de la bâtir sur Soneium, la blockchain de Sony (couche 2 d’Ethereum de Sony Block Solutions Labs), sous réserve des règles PlayStation. Aucun partenariat ni aucune approbation n’existe. Les contrats sont rédigés, mais ni déployés ni audités. Le jeu console est entièrement jouable sans cette couche.
